# Fable 5 Brain Vault Instructions

Read order:

1. `wiki/hot.md`
2. `wiki/index.md`
3. Relevant workflow, source, entity, decision, or deliverable note

Rules:

- Preserve `.raw/` as immutable source material.
- Use flat YAML frontmatter and wikilinks.
- Keep `hot`, `index`, `overview`, and `log` current.
- Keep recommendations source-cited.
- Do not store credentials, tokens, cookies, or private secrets.
- Do not make domain-specific claims without dated trustworthy sources.
- Apply `shipping-rules.md` before release-affecting changes.

Vault specifics: frontmatter and body contracts live in wiki/meta/CONVENTIONS.md; confidence policy in references/CONFIDENCE_TAGS.md; export quotes verbatim, at most 15 words, with line refs. No em dashes anywhere.
