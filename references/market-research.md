## Buyer Hypothesis

AI power users and teams who operate Claude Fable 5 inside Claude Code and need current, cited operating guidance

## Demand Questions

- Who pays for this workflow? Teams standardizing on Claude Code who cannot afford stale or invented model facts (pricing, refusal behavior, context limits) in their internal runbooks.
- What raw materials do they already have? System prompt exports, CLAUDE.md files, settings.json, changelogs, and scattered bookmarks of Anthropic docs.
- What output saves time or reduces risk? The capability matrix, the cited best-practices playbook, and the export diff report when Anthropic ships changes.
- What would they screenshot in a sales page? The Obsidian graph of 8 color-coded domains and a note answering a hard Fable 5 question with a dated official URL.
- What weekly or monthly loop brings them back? The refresh_due ledger discipline: sources expire, the brain flags them, the refresh flow re-verifies.
